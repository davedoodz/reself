Why speaking works

A thought you rehearse silently can run for hours without changing shape. Saying it out loud gives it a beginning and an end, and something to come back to that is not memory.

RE:SELF records what you say, keeps it on your phone, and lets you return to it. No transcription service, no account on a server, no feed. The research it draws on comes out of MIT and Harvard.
